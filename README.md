# 🛡️ GuardianXR

### AR-Based Vocational Safety Training | Smart India Hackathon 2026

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26041  
**Project:** GuardianXR  
**Theme:** Smart Education  
**Category:** Software

GuardianXR is an Android Augmented Reality (AR) safety-training application developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**. It creates virtual industrial emergency scenarios in the user's real environment and guides workers or trainees through practical, step-by-step safety procedures using an ARCore-supported Android phone.

The prototype focuses on **Fire & Explosion Response** and **Gas Leak & Confined Space Safety**, followed by assessment and QR-based certification.

## 🎯 Problem

Industrial safety training is often delivered through classroom sessions, presentations and videos. These methods provide limited hands-on experience, while conducting real fire or gas-leak drills can be risky, costly and difficult to repeat.

## 💡 Our Solution

GuardianXR uses a smartphone camera and AR to place simulated hazards in the real environment. The learner follows guided safety-response steps, completes an assessment and can receive a QR-based training certificate — without requiring a dedicated VR headset.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📱 Mobile AR-based industrial safety training
- 🔥 Fire & Explosion Response module
- ☣️ Gas Leak & Confined Space module
- 📍 ARCore surface detection and spatial hazard placement
- 🧭 Step-by-step emergency-response guidance
- 📝 Assessment after training modules
- 🎓 QR-based safety training certificate
- ✅ Offline certificate verification
- 🌐 English, Hindi and Santali language support
- 📊 Compliance dashboard for training records
- 🔄 Repeatable training workflow

## 🔥 Fire & Explosion Response

1. Scan the real-world environment.
2. Place the virtual fire hazard.
3. Follow safe approach and extinguisher procedures.
4. Confirm alarm and evacuation route.
5. Complete the response sequence.
6. Take the assessment and generate a certificate after passing.

## ☣️ Gas Leak & Confined Space

1. Scan the surrounding environment.
2. Place the virtual gas-leak hazard.
3. Establish an exclusion zone.
4. Perform PPE and buddy checks.
5. Confirm ventilation and a safe escape route.
6. Complete the response sequence and assessment.

## ⚙️ How GuardianXR Works

`Real Environment → ARCore Scan → Surface Detection → Hazard Placement → Guided Training → Assessment → QR Certificate → Offline Verification`

## 🚀 Run from Source Code

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the Android project in Android Studio.
3. Allow Gradle dependencies to sync.
4. Connect an ARCore-supported Android device.
5. Build and run the application.
6. Grant camera permission.
7. Select a training module and scan the environment.
8. Tap a detected surface to place the virtual hazard.

## 🧪 Prototype Validation

The GuardianXR prototype has been tested on a physical ARCore-capable Android device. The demonstrated workflow includes AR hazard placement, Fire and Gas training steps, assessments, QR certificate generation, offline certificate verification and the compliance dashboard.

## 🌍 Expected Impact

GuardianXR is designed to make industrial safety training more practical, accessible and repeatable. It allows trainees to practise emergency-response procedures without creating a real fire or gas hazard and can be extended with additional industrial training modules.

## ⚠️ Safety Note

GuardianXR is a **training simulator**. It is not a replacement for certified industrial safety procedures, qualified instructors, supervisors or emergency-response systems.
